# Phone Teardown Animation — Reference Prompt

## VIDEO PROMPT (for Runway Gen-3 / Pika / Sora)

### Scene Description
A premium dark background (near-black #0a0a12) with a subtle purple glow in the center. A realistic iPhone 15 Pro in Natural Titanium color sits center-frame, viewed from a slight front-angle. The phone is photorealistic — not a drawing, not CSS, not flat. It looks like a real product photo.

### Animation Sequence (15-20 seconds)

**Seconds 0-2:** Phone is stationary, slight ambient light shift. The phone looks like a real device you could hold.

**Seconds 2-5:** The screen/display assembly slowly lifts upward and tilts backward at ~30 degrees, as if someone is gently prying it off. You can see the thin adhesive edges. The screen floats above the body, revealing the internal chassis underneath — dark gray metal with visible screw holes, component slots, and flex cable traces.

**Seconds 5-8:** The battery (a dark purple rectangular cell with printed text "Li-ion 3274 mAh") smoothly slides out from the bottom-left of the phone body, moving to the right and slightly forward (toward the camera). It rotates gently as it floats. The empty battery compartment is now visible in the chassis.

**Seconds 8-11:** The camera module (a square block with 3 camera lenses and flash) lifts out from the top-right corner, moving diagonally up-right. The lenses catch light with blue-purple reflections as they separate. The empty camera bay with circular cutouts is revealed.

**Seconds 11-14:** The logic board (a green PCB with a visible chip labeled "A17 Pro" and tiny surface-mount components) slides out to the left, rotating slightly. Tiny gold connector pads glint.

**Seconds 14-16:** A small speaker/taptic engine module drops downward from the bottom.

**Seconds 16-18:** All separated components hover around the phone body in an exploded view — screen floating above, battery to the right, camera upper-right, logic board to the left, speaker below. The phone body (chassis + titanium frame) sits in the center with all empty slots visible.

**Seconds 18-20:** Smooth transition — all parts fly back into place. Phone reassembles. Screen settles back down. Brief glow/pulse effect on reassembly.

### Camera Movement
- Starts with a subtle slow orbit (like a product showcase turntable)
- Slight zoom-in as the screen lifts
- Camera pulls back slightly during the exploded view
- No hard cuts — everything is one continuous smooth shot

### Lighting
- Studio product photography lighting — soft key light from upper-left
- Subtle rim light on the phone edges (titanium catches light beautifully)
- The purple ambient glow behind the phone pulses gently
- Each component has subtle shadows as it separates from the body
- Camera lenses have specular highlights (bright blue-white reflection dots)

### Style
- Apple product page aesthetic — clean, premium, minimal
- Dark background (#0a0a12 or similar very dark blue-black)
- The phone looks photorealistic, not cartoon, not wireframe
- Smooth 60fps motion, no jitter
- Components have realistic depth and shadow as they separate
- Each part maintains its proper proportions and colors

### Quality
- 4K if possible
- Smooth motion, cinematic feel
- Product showcase quality

## KEY DETAILS TO EMPHASIZE

1. **The phone must look REAL** — titanium texture, glass reflections, proper proportions
2. **Screen lifts first** — revealing the internal chassis with empty slots
3. **Each component leaves its empty slot visible** — battery bay, camera bay, board area
4. **Components float in 3D space** — with proper shadows and depth
5. **Smooth continuous motion** — no jumps, no hard cuts
6. **Dark background** with subtle purple glow
7. **360-degree rotation** — the phone slowly rotates throughout, showing front, sides, back
8. **Reassembly at the end** — everything snaps back together
9. **Apple product page aesthetic** — clean, premium, minimal
